About 2017

The model goes north.

Talaris Conference Center · 180 attendees · 8 employers · Friends of Mongolia, title sponsor.

The 2017 edition brought the combined MACF+NHUB format to Seattle's Mongolian community over three days, March 18–20, at the Talaris Conference Center. The program ran as a coordinated weekend: Saturday's company tours at Amazon, Google, and Microsoft, Sunday's open discussion with a tech-leadership keynote and a four-speaker panel, and Monday's career fair with eight major employers and a "Хамтдаа Хийе" team case-study competition.

By the numbers: 220+ pre-registered, 180 in person at peak, 30 volunteers from 20+ cities working 9 teams over 7 months, 9,000+ volunteer hours, and 8 on-site interviews completed at the career fair.

The 2017 program

Three days, built around access.

Seattle's Mongolian tech network, Microsoft, Amazon, Google alumni, opened their workplaces for Saturday tours; Sunday brought the open discussion; Monday closed with a case-study competition and a career fair with recruiters from IBM, Microsoft, Dell, Amazon, Walgreens, RCF, B.J. Kane, and Publicis Media.

Day 01, Saturday, March 18 · Company tours

Saturday opened the Seattle weekend with parallel small-group tours of Amazon, Google's Fremont office, and Microsoft's Redmond campus, all hosted by each company's Mongolian alumni network.

  • Morning, Amazon company tour: hosted by Amarbayar Amarsanaa and Altantsetseg Batsaikhan from Amazon's Mongolian network. ~20 attendees. The room included Amazon's pet-friendly office details that became one of the most-talked-about parts of the weekend.
  • Morning, Google Fremont office tour: hosted by Batkhuyag Batsaikhan. Five attendees walked through Google's Lake Washington Canal-adjacent campus.
  • Morning, Microsoft Redmond tour: ~30 attendees, the largest tour group of the weekend. Hosted by six members of Microsoft's Mongolian Association on their day off, opened by Senior Software Manager Ulzii.
  • Afternoon, Seattle city tour: coordinated by MYUS (Seattle Mongolian Youth Association). Pike Place Market (the original Starbucks, the gum wall), Space Needle, and the University of Washington campus.
  • Evening, Speed Networking at The Chieftain: all-attendee dinner reception in a structured "Speed Networking" mixer format.

Day 02, Sunday, March 19 · Open discussion

Sunday's open discussion ("Нээлттэй Хэлэлцүүлэг") was the speaking centerpiece of the weekend. V. Bayarsaikhan keynoted with three threads: diligence, work–life balance, and rooting yourself in your community. The panel after him spanned software engineering, public administration, entrepreneurship, and finance.

  • Morning, Keynote, "America Success": V. Bayarsaikhan, at the time Product Manager at Dell Technologies, formerly Senior Product Manager at Amazon, currently CEO of And Global / Lend.mn. Three-part talk: hard work without giving up, work–life balance, and understanding the society around you.
  • Late morning, Open Discussion panel: a four-panelist conversation. On request from attendees, an additional "Women in the workplace" segment was opened during E. Bolormaa's portion.
  • Afternoon, Q&A & community: open floor with the panel and keynote speakers; continued discussion on careers, mentorship, and pathways back to Mongolia.

Day 03, Monday, March 20 · Career fair

Monday opened with the "Хамтдаа Хийе" (Let's Do It Together) case-study competition, the first 15 registered attendees split into three teams to prototype solutions to Mongolia's brain-drain problem.

  • Morning, "Хамтдаа Хийе" case study competition: three teams, Brain Exchange (winner), Open Box, Idea Hustlers. Each had 20 minutes to present a solution to Mongolia's brain-drain problem and 10 minutes for Q&A. Winner determined by audience vote via social media.
  • Afternoon, Career fair floor: eight major employers, IBM, Microsoft, Dell Technologies, Amazon, Walgreens, Resource Capital Funds, B.J. Kane & Co, Publicis Media. Sectors: technology, banking, finance, accounting, business, marketing, mining. Eight on-site interviews completed.
  • Evening, Closing reception: wrap-up with the team and the post-summit cohort. The 2018 board onboarding wave took shape in conversations here, including future MNG Summit President Yanjinlkham Munkhbat, who attended Seattle as a participant.
