Program 03

The Hackathon.

A first-of-its-kind, agentic-AI hackathon for Mongolian builders solving Mongolian and immigrant problems.

Visa paperwork. Document translation. Cross-border banking. Healthcare navigation. Career-search loops. Whatever the diaspora's most-Googled headache is, let's build it. Open to coders and no-coders alike. Prize pool funded by sponsors. Top three teams pitch at the after-party.
