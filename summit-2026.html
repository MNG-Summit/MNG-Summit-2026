Why this year

The most consequential Mongolian gathering of the year.

2026 marks a turning point. After a decade hosted across six American cities, MNG Summit plants a permanent flag in the city of immigrants and ambition: New York. With our new leadership team, Anu-Ujin Gereltod and Zanabazar Khatanbaatar, we've doubled our prep timeline, sharpened our program, and curated 100 seats for the people most likely to shape the next decade of the Mongolian diaspora.

Theater-style keynotes that move you. Panels that challenge you. Breakouts where you get pulled aside by someone whose career you've quietly admired for years. By the time the lights go up at the after-party, you'll have done a year's worth of relationship-building in one afternoon.

The 2026 program · Subject to change

Human Intelligence over AI.

The 2026 theme is Human Intelligence over Artificial Intelligence, a direct response to the most-asked question in our community: what does career mobility look like when AI is reshaping entry-level work, hiring, and the path from school to first job?

Program in development. Times, formats, and speakers below are placeholders being finalized through advisory and board sessions; final agenda publishes summer 2026.

Day 01 · Friday, October 9

Arrive in New York.
Find your people.

Day one is for context. Most attendees fly in. Welcome reception, an optional UN/diplomatic visit, and curated Mongolian dinners, small tables, real conversations, no nametags. Optional · no-pressure entry.

2:00 PM – 4:00 PM
UN delegation visit, Mongolia at the world stage
Optional behind-the-scenes tour with the Mongolian Mission to the UN. Limited to 25 attendees; first registered, first seated.
5:00 PM – 7:00 PM
Welcome reception, the soft open
Roof-deck venue in Midtown. Light bites, Mongolian music, name-card mixer organized by industry. Press, sponsors, and 2026 organizers attend.
7:30 PM – late
Mongolian dinners, five tables, five neighborhoods
Sign up for one of five hosted dinners across NYC. Eight seats per table. Themed by career stage (early-career, founders, executives, creatives, returning to Mongolia). The dinner is the event.
Day 02 · Saturday, October 10

Build something.
Learn something useful.

Saturday is the work day. NHUB project pitches in the morning, deep-dive workshops in the afternoon, the mentorship-cohort opening session in the evening. Workshop day · open to all attendees.

9:00 AM – 10:00 AM
Coffee, kickoff, and the day's frame
President Yanjinlkham Munkhbat opens with the year's framing question: what does the next decade of Mongolian impact look like, and which seat do you want in it?
10:00 AM – 12:00 PM
NHUB pitches, "Mongolians solving Mongolian problems"
Eight finalist projects pitch to a live judging panel for $25K in non-dilutive grants and a year of org-level support. Past winners include Breathe Mongolia.
12:00 PM – 1:30 PM
Lunch + structured networking
Industry-coded tables (tech, finance, climate, policy, creative, healthcare). Roving facilitators. You leave lunch with three new follow-ups in your calendar.
1:30 PM – 3:30 PM
Three deep-dive workshops, your pick
Track A: AI tools for builders (agentic, no-code, real workflows). Track B: Mental health & belonging in the diaspora. Track C: Founding & fundraising, pitch decks, term sheets, what actually closes.
3:30 PM – 5:00 PM
Hackathon kickoff, teams form, problems pitched
48-hour build challenge launches. Teams form on the spot. Themes drawn from real Mongolia-diaspora pain points sourced from the community ahead of time.
6:00 PM – 8:00 PM
Mentorship cohort opening, matching night
Mentors and mentees in the room together for the first time. Speed-matching format. The 2026 cohort starts here; pairings continue through 2027.
Day 03 · Sunday, October 11

The flagship.
The day on the poster.

Theater-style. ~100 seats. The keynote everyone's been waiting for, the panel that defines the year, the closing announcement that sets MNG Summit's next chapter. Main stage · capped at 100 attendees.

9:00 AM – 10:00 AM
Doors, coffee, and the final call
Curated arrival ritual. Seat assignments by industry pod. Optional 9:30 fireside chat with a 2024 alum.
10:00 AM – 10:45 AM
Opening keynote, to be announced
A Mongolian who's done it. Founder, investor, climate scientist, athlete, or storyteller. Booked for impact, not for clout.
10:45 AM – 12:00 PM
Panel, The career we're actually building
Five Mongolians. Five industries. One honest conversation about the climb, including the parts we don't say out loud.
12:00 PM – 1:30 PM
Lunch + hackathon demos
Sunday lunch is also the hackathon judging, Saturday's teams demo to the full room while everyone eats. Audience-vote prize, judges' prize, NHUB grant-track prize.
1:30 PM – 3:00 PM
Founder fireside, the unfiltered hour
Two Mongolian founders in conversation with a moderator who isn't afraid to ask the hard ones. No PR talking points. Off-the-record portion at the end.
3:00 PM – 4:00 PM
Closing keynote + year-ahead reveal
Where MNG Summit goes next. NYC chapter launch, mentorship cohort 2027 sign-up window, NHUB grant cycle dates, and the venue/date for Summit XI.
7:00 PM – late
Gala & after-party, where careers get made
Black-tie optional. Mongolian-catered. Live performance. The room you wish your network had every night, for one night.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will the 2026 summit sell out?
The 2024 summit sold out. The 2023 summit sold out. We expect 2026, our first NYC flagship, will sell out faster. Get on the early list now and we'll save you a seat at the price you saw today.
Is the program final?
No. The agenda, speakers, and venue are still being finalized with the advisory and working board, and details will continue to evolve as we lock partners and confirmations. The final agenda publishes summer 2026.
What if I can't afford a ticket?
Email scholarships@mngsummit.org. We have funded seats every year, no questions asked.
Can I apply to speak instead of attend?
Yes. If you've built, led, or learned something worth sharing, apply via get-involved.html#speak.
