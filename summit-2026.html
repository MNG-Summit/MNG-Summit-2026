MNG Summit 2026 is coming to New York City! This year's theme is Human Intelligence over AI and you will hear from industry leaders about how they're using AI tools to level up. We will also have creatives who are staying curious and building something meaningful.
So come join us and mingle with bright minds who are eager to learn and connect in this era of digitalization!
