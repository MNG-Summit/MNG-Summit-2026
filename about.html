About MNG Summit

We build the front door for the global Mongolian professional class.

Twelve years. Ten cities. Thousands of careers shaped. A volunteer-run 501(c)(3) for the next generation of Mongolians, anywhere on the map.

Our mission

To empower, encourage, and engage the next generation of Mongolians to reach their fullest potential.

01 / Empower

Professional development, on world-class footing.

We bring trainings, frameworks, and rooms that match what the best of the diaspora encounter at MIT, McKinsey, or Sequoia, translated for our context, our communities, our careers.

02 / Encourage

A safe stage for ideas worth saying out loud.

From "I want to start a company" to "I want to come back home," our people say things at MNG Summit they don't say anywhere else. We protect that.

03 / Engage

Leadership built, not announced.

Every speaker we put on stage was once someone in our audience. The pipeline is the program. The program is the pipeline.
