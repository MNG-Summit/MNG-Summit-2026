About 2018

The team grew. The story got documented.

Los Angeles · 5th summit · MACF Anniversary Magazine published.

2018 brought MNG Summit south to Los Angeles for its fifth annual program. The year's headline artifact was the first MACF Anniversary Magazine: a printed (and Issuu-uploaded) retrospective of the org's first four years, designed to brief new sponsors and prospective partners.

Eight new committee and board members onboarded that fall, including future President Yanjinlkham Munkhbat, who joined the Design team after attending the 2017 Seattle summit as a participant. Programs, photos, and speaker names from the LA edition are still being reconstructed. Send anything you have to archive@mngsummit.org.

The 2018 program

A Southern California weekend.

Year five. First Southern California chapter. The program coincided with the publication of the MACF Anniversary Magazine, the first printed retrospective of the org, and the onboarding of eight new committee and board members who would carry MNG Summit into the NYC era.

Day 00, Friday · Tour & arrival

Hotel check-in, an optional small-group walk through Hollywood and the LA arts district, and an evening welcome reception. Programs and timing for 2018 are still being reconstructed from the team's archive.

Day 01, Saturday · Career & storytelling

The Saturday main day featured the standard MNG Summit format: keynote, two panels (career journey and US-Mongolia opportunities), workshops on professional development, and the unveiling of the first MACF anniversary magazine.

  • Morning, Opening & keynote: welcome remarks from the MNG Summit board, followed by a keynote on career development.
  • Late morning, MACF anniversary magazine launch: first printed retrospective, four years of programming, sponsors, and alumni profiles. Distributed at the venue and uploaded to Issuu.
  • Afternoon, Panels & workshops: career journey panel, US-Mongolia opportunities panel, and parallel workshops on professional development. Speaker list still being reconstructed.

Day 02, Sunday · NHUB finals

The final day featured the project competition, "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна", with cohort finalists pitching solutions to issues facing Mongolia and the diaspora. The 2018 cohort included Erdemt, whose pitch deck (preserved on Drive) opened the conversation about Ulaanbaatar's social-impact pipeline.

  • Morning, Project presentations: NHUB finalists presented to a live judging panel.
  • Afternoon, Awards & closing: winners announced; the cohort continued through the year as alumni mentors.

2018 LA program is still being reconstructed from the MACF anniversary magazine and team archives. Help fill the gaps: archive@mngsummit.org.
