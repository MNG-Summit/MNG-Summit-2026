Summit VII · 2022 · Returning live

2022, Boston, Future of Work.

After a two-year pandemic pause, MNG Summit returned in person at District Hall in Boston: three days centered on the future of work, the "My Career Journey" panel of three MIT / Oxford / Harvard alumni, and the "I Have a Voice" student-team competition led by Harvard's Future of Education student club.

About 2022

The summit that brought everyone back.

District Hall · Harvard & MIT tour · NHUB cohort · 7th annual, post-COVID return.

Programming spanned three days. Friday was an optional walking tour of Harvard, MIT, the Freedom Trail, and Acorn Street, led by Mongolian-American students from those campuses. Saturday opened with Khatantuul Zorig's keynote on AI through a founder lens, Anar Chinbaatar on Mongolia's IT industry, and Uyanga Ayurzana of Harvard's Future of Education student club kicking off the "I Have a Voice" team competition.

The "My Career Journey" panel paired three MIT, Oxford, and Harvard alumni: Tana Jambaldorj from McKinsey, Uuganbayar Otgonbaatar from Exelon, and Namuun Purevdorj from Heyday, moderated by Marla Munkh-Achit. Sunday brought a career-direction talk from Nomin Jargalsaikhan (Whitstable Consulting), a "Begin Your Career with the End in Mind" workshop from Undram Boldbat (IMF), Zolbayar Enkhbaatar of Lemon Press on the future of Mongolian business, and the "I Have a Voice" award ceremony.

The 2022 program

Three days, built
around the comeback.

The first in-person summit since 2019. Three days at District Hall in Boston's Seaport, a Friday tour, a Saturday main stage anchored by Khatantuul Zorig's AI keynote and the "My Career Journey" panel, and a Sunday of career-path workshops and the "I Have a Voice" awards.

Day 00 · Friday, March 18

Walk Boston with Mongolian-American students. Optional Friday tour day, Mongolian students from Harvard and MIT led small-group walks through historic Boston and the two campuses, followed by lunch and an afternoon along the Freedom Trail and Acorn Street.

9:00 AM · Harvard University tour
Student-led campus walk, including the Mongolian student community on campus.
11:00 AM · MIT tour
Bus commute from Harvard, then student-led MIT walk-through.
12:30 PM · Group lunch
Casual lunch in Cambridge before the afternoon walk.
1:15 PM · Freedom Trail & Acorn Street
Afternoon walking tour through Beacon Hill's most photographed streets.
5:00 PM · Back to the hotel
Free evening; informal meet-ups in the hotel lobby.

Day 01 · Saturday, March 19

AI, careers, and the student-club track. The main day at District Hall in Boston's Seaport, Khatantuul Zorig keynoted on AI through a founder lens, Anar Chinbaatar spoke on Mongolia's IT industry, and the "My Career Journey" panel paired McKinsey, Exelon, and Heyday.

8:30 AM · Registration & refreshments
Coffee, light breakfast, name-badge pickup at District Hall.
9:30 AM · Keynote: Future of Work
Khatantuul "Khat" Zorig, investor at Innospark Ventures (a Boston-based VC backing early-stage purpose-driven AI companies). MBA, MIT Sloan, focused on entrepreneurship, AI, and social impact. First Mongolian at MIT Sloan and first Mongolian VC. Talk: AI and the future of work in healthcare, life sciences, wellness, and education.
10:30 AM · Guest speaker: Mongolian IT industry
Anar Chinbaatar, Mongolian information technology industry leader. Specialist in integrated systems for affordable telecommunications. Started his career as a lead engineer at the Mongolian Telecommunications Company at 19; in 2016, founded a company building mobile-network infrastructure to lower communication costs in Mongolia.
11:45 AM · "I Have a Voice", team distribution
Uyanga Ayurzana, Future of Education student-club organizer, Harvard Students' Club. Kicked off the NHUB-style team competition for student teams to design solutions for the next 24 hours.
12:00 PM · Lunch & networking
First in-person lunch in years. The room felt it.
1:00 PM · Workshop: Design Thinking for Remote Work
Facilitated by Uyanga Ayurzana. A 2.5-hour applied design-thinking session on remote-work challenges, with stationery and structured group exercises.
1:30 PM · Panel: My Career Journey
Three panelists, one moderator. Tana Jambaldorj (McKinsey · former Rio Tinto, Oyu Tolgoi · Oxford MBA, Harvard AB) · Uuganbayar "Ugi" Otgonbaatar (Sr. Corporate Strategy, Exelon · BS & PhD Nuclear Engineering, MIT) · Namuun Purevdorj (Sr. Manager, Heyday · MASc MIT). Moderated by Marla Munkh-Achit.
All day · Booths: resume, mock interview, LinkedIn portraits
Resume review (four board members by industry), mock interview, LinkedIn headshot booth, plus Breathe Mongolia and Lemon Press / Inside Mongolia exhibitor booths.

Day 02 · Sunday, March 20

Career path. "I Have a Voice" awards. Sunday opened with Saturday's student-club teams presenting their solutions, career-direction talks and workshops, and closed with the "I Have a Voice" award ceremony.

9:00 AM · Registration & refreshments
Light breakfast.
10:00 AM · "I Have a Voice", team presentations
Cohort teams presented their 24-hour solutions to a panel of judges including Uyanga Ayurzana and the Future of Education student-club organizers from Harvard.
11:00 AM · Guest speaker: Define Your Career
Nomin Jargalsaikhan, Founder & Executive Director of Whitstable Consulting. ICF Professional Certified Coach. Framework for defining career direction in the early-mid stage.
11:40 AM · Workshop: Begin Your Career with the End in Mind
Undram Boldbat, HR & Project Management Professional at the International Monetary Fund. Tips on building a successful resume, networking, online visibility, salary negotiation, and working with the end in mind once you've landed the job.
12:40 PM · Lunch & networking
Industry mixers continue.
1:40 PM · Guest speaker: Future of Business in Mongolia
Zolbayar Enkhbaatar, Co-Founder & Editor-in-Chief at Lemon Press. Launched Inside Mongolia to promote Mongolia to the world. Pursuing an Economics master's at NYU. Founded Mongolia's first mutual fund, "National Privatization Fund," in 2020.
2:25 PM · "I Have a Voice", award ceremony
Winners announced. The first in-person NHUB-style awards since 2019.

The stage

The 2022 speakers.

First in-person summit since 2019. Speaker bios verified against the live mngsummit.org/bostonsummit page; titles as of March 2022.

Keynote · AI through a founder lens

K
Keynote
Khatantuul "Khat" Zorig
Investor at Innospark Ventures, a Boston-based VC backing early-stage purpose-driven AI companies in New England. MBA, MIT Sloan, focused on entrepreneurship, AI, and social impact. First Mongolian at MIT Sloan and first Mongolian VC. Born and raised in Mongolia, immigrated alone at 16. Co-led investments at Innospark across healthcare, life sciences, wellness, and education. Cares deeply about supporting underdog founders in emerging economies.

Three guest talks · Across both days

A
Mongolian IT industry
Anar Chinbaatar
Mongolian information technology industry leader and specialist in integrated systems for affordable telecommunications and IT services. Started as a lead engineer at the Mongolian Telecommunications Company at 19. Developed and manufactured firewalls for local organizations. In 2016, founded a company building mobile-network infrastructure to lower mobile communication costs in Mongolia.
N
Career direction
Nomin Jargalsaikhan
Founder and Executive Director of Whitstable Consulting. UK Registered Career Development Professional and an International Member of the American Psychological Association. ICF Professional Certified Coach (PCC). Previously with BTS Coach and Shackleton Group, where she co-led the expansion into coaching and training, working with Rio Tinto, Jacobs, Samsung, and Ernst & Young. Former Director of Mongolia Talent Network, the country's leading recruitment firm.
Z
Mongolian business
Zolbayar Enkhbaatar
Co-Founder and Editor-in-Chief at Lemon Press, a leading financial media company in Mongolia. Launched Inside Mongolia earlier in the year to promote Mongolia to the world. Manages the editorial team from New York while pursuing an Economics master's at NYU. Started his career at an economics research think-tank in Mongolia, then established Mongolia's first mutual fund, "National Privatization Fund," in 2020.

Panel · "My Career Journey"

T
Panelist · Consulting
Tana Jambaldorj
Engagement Manager at McKinsey & Company, focusing on metals & mining and sustainability. Previously with Oyu Tolgoi in Mongolia and Rio Tinto in Australia. Co-founder and head of the advisory board for the Mongolian Young Scholars Program. MBA, Oxford Saïd Business School (WEF Global Shapers Community Scholar); AB in Economics & East Asian Studies, Harvard.
U
Panelist · Energy
Uuganbayar "Ugi" Otgonbaatar
Sr. Corporate Strategy Manager at Exelon Corporation. Since 2016, has worked on Exelon's corporate R&D Partnership program for early-stage energy: clean hydrogen generation, energy storage, and technologies for repurposing the existing nuclear fleet. BS & PhD in Nuclear Science & Engineering, MIT. Prior R&D at Électricité de France, General Electric, and Tokyo Electric Power.
N
Panelist · Supply chain
Namuun Purevdorj
Sr. Manager of Supply Chain & Operations at Heyday, a unicorn consumer-products startup. Leads Global Sourcing & Supplier Development across the Americas and EU and spearheads the company's supply chain sustainability initiatives.
M
Moderator
Marla Munkh-Achit
MNG Summit Marketing & Communications Director at the time. Obama Foundation Scholar, Columbia.
