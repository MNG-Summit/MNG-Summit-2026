Programs · Coming 2026

MNG Hub.
The digital platform
powering it all.

A platform built for the diaspora: mentorship matching, a member directory, year-round community engagement, and the tools to keep the room together when nobody's in the same city.

Launches 2026 Includes Mentorship matching Membership Free for nomads

What it does.

  • Mentorship matching: Hand-supplemented matching engine. Industry, career stage, and time-zone. Three-month cohorts.
  • Member directory: Every alum + speaker + working board member, searchable.
  • Community feed: A members-only space for jobs, intros, asks.
  • Event tools: RSVPs, attendee networking, post-event follow-ups.

Why we're building it.

For twelve years we've gathered once a year and the conversations carried us until the next summit. Now the network is too big and too distributed to wait for October.

The Hub is the biggest infrastructure bet in a decade — the place the room lives between summits, owned by the community, not by Meta or LinkedIn.

Mentorship · the first feature live.

The mentorship program runs entirely on MNG Hub. Three-month cohorts, hand-matched by industry, career stage, and time-zone. Two hours a month. Real outcomes.

  • Time commitment: Two hours a month for three months.
  • What we ask: A short intake form and one onboarding call.
  • What you get: A second-degree network you can actually call.
