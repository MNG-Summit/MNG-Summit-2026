About 2019

The last summit before the world stopped.

New York City · 6th annual · Breathe Mongolia · NHUB.

2019 brought MNG Summit to New York City for the first time. The NHUB project competition that year produced one of the org's most consequential alumni, the Breathe Mongolia air-quality awareness campaign, which spun out as a standalone initiative addressing Ulaanbaatar's pollution crisis.

It was also the last summit before the COVID-19 pandemic forced a two-year pause; programming would not resume in person until Boston in 2022. Programs, photos, and speaker names are still being collected, send them to archive@mngsummit.org.

The 2019 program

Four days, built around the city.

2019 was the first NYC edition. The four-day program paired Manhattan tours with a full panel-and-workshop day, capped by the NHUB project competition and a film screening. The room included Mongolia's Permanent Representative to the UN; the closer announced the launch of MNG Hub.

Day 00, Friday, March 22 · Arrival & check-in

Arrival day for out-of-town attendees. Hotel check-in opened at 3:00 PM, with informal meet-ups in the lobby and MNG Summit badge pickup at the front desk.

Day 01, Saturday, March 23 · NYC tour day

A full day of curated New York. Three parallel small-group tours ran at 10:00 AM: Tour A, Wall Street insider, a Financial District walking tour led by a working professional; Tour B, United Nations Headquarters, a behind-the-scenes tour organized with the Mongolian Mission to the UN; and Tour C, American Museum of Natural History, featuring the Mongolia paleontology collection. Lunch at Chelsea Market (12:30 PM) was followed by a High Line walk, a Times Square & Rockefeller Center afternoon (2:00 PM) with a cohort photo at Rockefeller Plaza, and a 7:00 PM networking night at Elsie Rooftop, 1412 Broadway, Midtown, the first MNG Summit rooftop event in NYC.

Day 02, Sunday, March 24 · Panels & workshops

The main day opened with the Mongolian Ambassador to the United Nations and closed with the launch announcement for MNG Hub.

  • 8:30 AM, Registration & refreshments: coffee, light breakfast, name-badge pickup.
  • 9:00 AM, Opening ceremony: Mongolian national anthem, followed by a Morin Khuur performance by Bayarjargal Chogsom.
  • 9:15 AM, Introductory remarks: Nominsuren Munkhuu, President of MNG Summit.
  • 9:30 AM, Welcoming remarks: H.E. Mr. Sukhbold Sukhee, Ambassador and Permanent Representative of Mongolia to the United Nations.
  • 9:45 AM, Keynote, "Personal technological advancement": Sukhbat Lkhagvadorj, Co-founder of Coinseed and founder of the Association of Mongolian Students in America (AMSA).
  • 11:00 AM, Top 5 US companies for young professionals: Batkhaan Namshir, International Trade Analyst, Microsoft.
  • 11:30 AM, Top 5 Mongolian companies for young professionals: Munkhzul Bayanmunkh (COO, Retoch LLC) and Amarbayar Amarsanaa (Senior SRE, GitLab Inc).
  • 12:00 PM, Lunch & networking: box lunches; tables organized by industry.
  • 1:00 PM, Workshop, "Social media for brands vs. personal career": Bil G (Beatmaker & Social Media Manager) and Batchimeg Ganbaatar (Digital Strategist, The Boeing Company).
  • 1:45 PM, MNG Hub launch announcement: first public introduction of the MNG Hub concept by Batchimeg Ganbaatar, the platform that would become the org's year-round community vehicle.
  • 2:00 PM, Panel, "My career journey in the United States": Nomi Ganbold (Entrepreneur & Producer), Urgamal Azat (Founder, Oddariya Foundation), Enkhtaivan Oyunbazar (Financial Analyst, GlobeTax Services), Ider Batbayar (Research Analyst Consultant, UNDP). Moderated by Enkhtuya Enkh-Erdene (Senior Accountant, Walgreens).
  • 3:00 PM, "Life Post-F1" Q&A: Duka Baatarchuluun, Immigration Attorney at DB Associates, LTD.
  • 3:45 PM, Career mentorship program introduction: Nominsuren Munkhuu; sign-up for the post-summit mentorship cohort.
  • 4:00 PM, Employment opportunities with the Government of Mongolia: career pathways for Mongolian professionals in the US considering a return.
  • 7:00 PM, Networking at Tibet House US: closing reception at the cultural center.

Day 03, Monday, March 25 · NHUB finals

Day three was dedicated to "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна", the project competition that launched as MNG Summit's flagship social-impact program.

  • 10:00 AM, Registration & refreshments: light breakfast.
  • 10:30 AM, NHUB introductory remarks: D. Jantsankhorol (Jantsankhorol Damdinsuren), NHUB Committee Lead.
  • 10:45 AM, Special remarks: G. Galbadrakh (Galbadrakh Gombosuren), Author and Head Judge of the NHUB competition.
  • 11:00 AM, Project presentation I: first NHUB finalist pitches the cohort.
  • 11:30 AM, Project presentation II: second NHUB finalist.
  • 12:00 PM, Lunch: light networking lunch with judges available for office hours.
  • 12:45 PM, Project presentation III: final NHUB finalist of the day.
  • 1:30 PM, Film screening, Blue Gold: documentary by UNDP Mongolia on water resources, followed by a short Q&A.
  • 2:00 PM, Closing remarks & presentation of awards: NHUB winners announced. The 2019 cohort included the team that would spin out as Breathe Mongolia, the air-quality awareness initiative addressing Ulaanbaatar's pollution crisis.
