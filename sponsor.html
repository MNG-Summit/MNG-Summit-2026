For funders

Three reasons MNG Summit is worth your foundation's attention.

01 / Returns

A diaspora investment with measurable returns.

Backing MNG Summit means backing the bridge between Mongolia and the global economy. Our alumni become the policy analysts, founders, climate writers, and bankers who reshape the country's connection to the world. Few interventions touch this many high-leverage individuals at this stage of their careers.

02 / Lean

A volunteer-led org that runs lean.

Zero salaried staff. A board of working professionals donating their nights and weekends. When you fund MNG Summit, your dollars don't disappear into operations, they fund speakers, venues, scholarships, and free seats for the students who need them most.

03 / Momentum

A 2026 moment that will set our trajectory.

This is the year we move from one annual summit to a year-round platform: monthly webinars, a hackathon, a mentorship program, and a permanent NYC chapter. Foundations entering this year join at the moment we are most leverage-able, and most visible.
