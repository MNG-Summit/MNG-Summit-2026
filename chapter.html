Program 05

NYC Chapter.

A formal New York chapter, monthly events, member benefits, mutual aid, and a clear front door for any Mongolian arriving in the city.

Networking dinners, panels, run clubs, hiking, an annual Mongolian New Year gala. Member benefits include partner discounts, scholarship eligibility, mentorship priority, and curated job-board access. The first chapter we've ever formalized, and the model for the chapters that come after, in cities your friends are already building toward.
