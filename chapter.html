NYC Chapter.
Find your people.
In New York.

A formal New York chapter launching in 2026. Monthly events, member benefits, mutual aid, and a permanent room.

Launches Spring 2026 Cadence Monthly events Membership Free, opt-in

What we're building.

  • Monthly meetups: First Thursday of every month, rotating Manhattan venues.
  • Industry circles: Quarterly dinners by sector: finance, tech, design, policy.
  • Co-working days: Show up, plug in, get together and grow. Open laptops, shared coffee, side-by-side hours.
  • Happy hours + games nights: Low-stakes ways to meet the room: pickleball, board-game nights, post-work drinks.
  • Mutual aid coming soon: A members-only board for jobs, housing, intros.
  • Welcome desk: If you're new to New York, we'll get you into the network in a week.

Why chapters.
Why now.

For twelve years MNG Summit lived once a year. A weekend. A photo. A LinkedIn post. And then back to inboxes. The community is bigger than a weekend now, and one annual room can't carry that weight.

So we're branching out. Chapters are how MNG Summit lives in your week, not just on your calendar.

We're starting with New York because New York made us. This city compresses people. It pushes harder, costs more, asks more, and somehow that's exactly what made the Mongolian network here pull tighter, not looser. Happy hours that turned into co-founder calls. Pickleball games that turned into reference checks. A board-game night that found someone a sublet.

None of that scales from a single annual summit. It scales from showing up to the same room, on the same Thursday, with the same people, every month.

NYC is door one. coming SF, Boston, Chicago, DC, Ulaanbaatar — if you're in a city with a few hundred Mongolian professionals and you want a chapter, tell us. We'll help you light it up.
