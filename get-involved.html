Common questions

Before you apply.

Still unsure where you fit? Email us and we'll point you to the right door.

When do speaker applications close?
Speaker applications close July 31, 2026. We respond within two weeks of submission — no essays required.
Do I need to be a celebrity to speak?
No. We don't book celebrities. We book the Mongolian no one's talking about yet — the operator, the founder, the practitioner — and put them on a stage, often for the first time.
How much time does volunteering take?
Most volunteers start with a 5–10 hour weekly commitment. We accept rolling applications and move people between teams as the year unfolds.
Do I have to be in New York to volunteer?
No. We hire by skill, not location. We have volunteers across multiple countries and timezones — that's why the volunteer form asks for your city and timezone.
What does the mentorship program involve?
Mentors are hand-matched with rising Mongolian professionals for a three-month cohort focused on real outcomes. See the Programs page for details.
