Understanding your legal rights. Bayarjargal helps our community navigate the legal system with confidence, especially as immigration policy keeps shifting. Licensed in Illinois since 2013 and admitted to federal court and the USPTO Bar, he covers immigration, criminal defense, real estate, estate planning, personal injury, and patent/trademark law. His Saturday workshop walks through common visa issues and paths.

Work & academia in the US. Bindi founded Structura Economics, modeling how energy and climate shocks ripple through markets and households; at the World Bank she co-developed MINDSET, a global simulation model used by governments and researchers. On the Saturday panel she'll cover AI in academic research and policy analysis, plus her path through US academia as an immigrant. She's also a DC-based stand-up comedian — catch her set at the Happy Hour.
LinkedIn →
Finding your voice on the page. Bayasgalan is a writer, poet, and translator pursuing an MFA in Creative Writing at Columbia. She founded Tagtaa Publishing, one of Mongolia's leading publishing houses, and is one of the country's most prominent literary translators — an International Writing Program alum (Iowa, 2018), a Frankfurt Book Fair Fellow (2019), and translation editor of Columbia Journal. Her Sunday workshop covers finding your voice, shaping a story, and building a practice that lasts. Bring a notebook.
Finding your own path. Ogo is a Staff Product Designer at Remote, a global HR platform for hiring and managing teams anywhere in the world, and turns complex systems into simple, human-centered experiences while exploring how AI is reshaping design and work. On the Saturday panel, she'll share how she built a career in the US without a roadmap — and why it comes down to owning your story, asking for help, and choosing work that supports the life you want.
Making AI work for you. Yulkendy is a Forbes 30 Under 30 social entrepreneur, TED speaker, and co-founder of Healers Health, building tech infrastructure for the social care of youth and families. A certified Google Coach, her Saturday workshop shows you how to put AI to work on the tasks that eat up your time — drafting, research, reports — using free, practical tools anyone can pick up, no experience required.
