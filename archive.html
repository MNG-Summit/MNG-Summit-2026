A note before you browse

Started as a career fair. Became a movement.

Est. 2014 · Volunteer-run · Always recruiting.

MNG Summit began life in 2014 as the Mongolian American Career Fair (MACF) in Chicago, a scrappy, all-volunteer effort to connect Mongolian students in the U.S. with companies that would actually hire them. By 2016, MACF merged with NHUB (Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна, the speech competition that had been running since 2011) to become the Mongolian Next Generation Summit. Two events, one weekend, one community.

Twelve years later, the through-line is unchanged: empower, encourage, engage. Every page in this collection was earned by the people who showed up to build it. Some details below come from our records, some from the way the internet remembered us. If you spot something missing, email contact@mngsummit.org and we'll fix it.

Nine chapters.
Pick one.

Every chapter from the seed in Chicago to the tenth-anniversary retreat in San Francisco. A pandemic pause from 2020–2021 didn't stop us. Click any year to step inside.

Summit I 2014 Chicago, IL The first MACF career fair, the movement began. Founded by five Mongolians from four continents. Summit II 2015 Washington, D.C. East coast. Policy. Public service. Organizing team widens; a distributed network forms. Summit III 2016 San Francisco, CA MACF + NHUB merge, the first "MNG Summit." Mar 18–20, Sandbox Suites · free to attend. Summit IV 2017 Seattle, WA Pacific Northwest chapter. 180 attendees · Amazon · Google · Microsoft tours. Summit V 2018 Los Angeles, CA Storytelling, screen, sun. First MACF anniversary magazine. Eight new committee members onboarded. Summit VI 2019 New York City, NY Our first NYC gathering, finance & ambition. The last pre-COVID summit. Summit VII 2022 Boston, MA Future of Work. Returning live. Mar 18–20, District Hall · Khat (MIT Sloan) keynote. Summit VIII 2023 Miami, FL Building Resilience, careers, cultures, capital. Mar 4–5, The Bass. Summit IX 2024 San Francisco, CA 10th Anniversary · United for Impact. Mar 15–17, USF McLaren · DJ BEME closer.
