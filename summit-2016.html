Past Summit · 2016

San Francisco, the first "MNG Summit."

The year MACF (Mongolian American Career Fair, est. 2014) and NHUB (Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна, the speech competition then in its 6th year) officially merged into one weekend-long gathering: the Mongolian Next Generation Summit.

About 2016

Two events. One weekend. The model that stuck.

Sandbox Suites SF · 185 attendees · 12 employers · free to attend. 3rd MACF · 6th NHUB · 1st combined.

For the first time, the Mongolian American Career Fair and the "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна" speech competition shared a single program: three days, one venue, one community. The 2016 edition was free to attend by deliberate choice, to maximize student access. The format established the template every subsequent summit built on.

Day one ran the Bay Area tech tour at Cisco and Facebook. Day two ran NHUB's 6th edition: eight finalists pitched solutions to Mongolia's challenges, followed by the "Employment in Mongolia" panel with four leaders from the state and private labor sectors. Day three was the career fair with twelve American and Mongolian employers, anchored by a remote keynote from Lhamour founder D. Khulan, beamed in live from Ulaanbaatar across a 14-hour time difference.

The 2016 program

Three days, one community.

The first edition that combined the Mongolian American Career Fair (MACF, 3rd edition) with the "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна" speech competition (NHUB, 6th edition) into a single weekend program. Free to attend by deliberate choice, to maximize student access.

Day 00, Friday, March 18 · Welcome day

Bay Area tech tour day. Facebook and Cisco HQ visits in the morning, networking lunch, an SF group tour in the afternoon, and an evening cocktail reception.

  • Morning, Cisco HQ tour: hosted by Guen, a Cisco engineer, who walked attendees through the lobby, cafeteria, conference rooms, his team's workspace, and the data center. Includes Cisco's on-campus housing, childcare, and fitness facilities for staff.
  • Morning, Facebook HQ tour: hosted by software engineers E. Khongor and O. Ulziibayar. ~10 attendees walked the Menlo Park campus, the ice-cream cart, the gaming room, the central lounge, and a long working lunch with both hosts on how they joined Facebook.
  • Afternoon, San Francisco group tour: walking tour of SF landmarks for first-time visitors, coordinated by the California Mongolian Youth Association (MYUC).
  • 8:00 PM, Cocktail reception: soft open. The first faces of the weekend.

Day 01, Saturday, March 19 · NHUB presentations

Eight finalists pitched solutions to issues at home, judged on eight criteria across 200 points. Indiana University's E. Saruul won with 191. The afternoon brought the "Employment in Mongolia" panel.

  • Morning, NHUB 6 speech competition: eight finalists. Each scored across eight criteria, problem definition, real-world feasibility, scientific evidence, data, conclusion, research, and delivery. Up to 200 points possible.
  • Late morning, Winner announced, E. Saruul: Indiana University. 191 out of 200 points.
  • Afternoon, Open Discussion, "Employment in Mongolia": a four-panelist conversation on the post-summit job market with T. Bilegee (CEO, Erdenes Tavan Tolgoi), G. Gerelmaa (Director, Capital City Department of Labor), Ts. Erdenebaatar (Mongolian Employers' Federation), and B. Belgutei (Director, Youth Employment Central Bureau).

Day 02, Sunday, March 20 · Career fair

The career fair itself: twelve American and Mongolian employers, mock interviews, and a remote keynote from Lhamour founder D. Khulan. Workshops ran in parallel on resume writing, interview prep, and US work authorization.

  • Morning, Remote keynote, D. Khulan: Founder, Lhamour · Director, Natural Essentials LLC. Joined live from Ulaanbaatar across a 14-hour time difference. Three threads: how to start a venture, what hurdles to expect, and how to stay true to a dream.
  • Late morning, Career fair floor opens: twelve employers, spanning American + Mongolian, private, state, and nonprofit. IBM, Erdenes Tavan Tolgoi, PwC, and nine others.
  • Afternoon, Workshop, resume & CV writing: free, parallel session on resume writing and feedback.
  • Afternoon, Workshop, interview prep: mock interview practice for students walking the career fair floor.
  • Afternoon, Workshop, OPT / H1B navigation: free session on US work-authorization pathways for international students.
  • Evening, Closing reception: end of the first combined MACF + NHUB weekend, the template that every subsequent summit built on.
