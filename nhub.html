Program 01 · On hiatus

NHUB.

Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна.

"I have something to say." · Project competition · Since 2011.

NHUB was MNG Summit's annual project competition, seed funding plus a stage for Mongolian youth with an idea worth building and the conviction to ship it. The program ran annually through 2019 (when the cohort produced Breathe Mongolia) and continued through the pandemic in modified form. The most recent stage iteration was the SF 2024 Open Mic at the McLaren Conference Center, which carried forward the "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна" tradition of community storytelling.

NHUB is currently on hiatus. We're not accepting new project applications for 2026. The team is restructuring the program for a future relaunch with stronger seed funding, dedicated mentorship, and a clearer post-summit accountability cycle. Past projects continue to compound in the work alumni keep doing.

2019 · NYC, The Breathe Mongolia cohort

The most consequential NHUB cohort in the org's history. Day 3 of the 2019 NYC summit (Monday, March 25) was dedicated to "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна" with three project presentations, a film screening of UNDP Mongolia's Blue Gold, and the awards. The cohort included the team that spun out as Breathe Mongolia, the air-quality awareness campaign that became a standalone initiative addressing Ulaanbaatar's pollution crisis, and is now a community partner that exhibited at every subsequent summit. NHUB Committee Lead: Jantsankhorol Damdinsuren · Head Judge: Galbadrakh Gombosuren (author).

2016 · SF, The 6th edition, first combined with MACF

2016 was the year MACF and NHUB merged into a single weekend program. Eight finalists pitched solutions to Mongolia's challenges, judged across eight criteria for up to 200 total points. E. Saruul (Indiana University) won with 191 out of 200. The day also featured the "Employment in Mongolia" open-discussion panel with leaders from Erdenes Tavan Tolgoi, the Capital City Department of Labor, the Mongolian Employers' Federation, and the Youth Employment Central Bureau.

2017 · Seattle, The case-study year

2017 paired the NHUB tradition with a Monday case-study competition ("Хамтдаа Хийе", Let's Do It Together) where the first fifteen registered attendees split into three teams to design solutions to Mongolia's brain-drain problem. Winning team: Brain Exchange. The Saturday discussion-stage hosted a four-speaker panel spanning software (Ulziidelger, Microsoft), public administration (Bolormaa, World Bank), entrepreneurship (Khishigbayar Tomorbaatar, Pico Greetings), and finance (Temuulen Tumurbat, RCF).

2022 · Boston, "I Have a Voice," student-club edition

The 2022 Boston revival ran NHUB as a 24-hour team competition called "I Have a Voice," kicked off by Uyanga Ayurzana of Harvard's Future of Education student club. Saturday afternoon teams formed and prototyped overnight; Sunday morning teams presented to a panel of student-club judges; the award ceremony closed the program before the Ministry of Education bridge session.

2024 · SF, The Open Mic, latest iteration

The 10th-anniversary SF summit reframed NHUB as Open Mic: "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна", community storytelling in Mongolian and English across both days. Speakers included Tegshee Baasan (Unlock Podcast), Urga Azat (DEM Foundation), Ariunzayat Yunren (esportsnews.mn), Batsoyombo Ts. (Bay Area Mongolian Community Association), Badmaasambuu Tsendayush (Vivi), Enkhhuslen Baterdene (Duke Public Policy), and Nyamjargal Lhagva (Amina Project). This was the most recent iteration of the NHUB tradition.

2018 · LA, Year five, NHUB on the program

The Los Angeles edition included the NHUB project competition track. Specific finalist names from 2018 are still being reconstructed from the team archive, photos, and the MACF Anniversary Magazine published that year.

2023 · Miami, "I Have a Voice" at Building Resilience

The Miami program ran the NHUB / "I Have a Voice" cohort alongside the Building Resilience theme, with workshops on stress management, cultural transition, and career restart. Specific 2023 NHUB finalist names are still being reconstructed.

2011–2015 · Origins, The independent speech competition

NHUB started in 2011 as an independent speech competition: Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна, "I Have Something to Say." It ran for five editions independently before merging with the Mongolian American Career Fair (MACF) in 2016 to become the combined MNG Summit format. Records from the early years live in the original organizers' personal archives; help us reconstruct: archive@mngsummit.org.
