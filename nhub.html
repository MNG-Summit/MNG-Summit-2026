NHUB is the speech and project competition that started it all. "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна," "I have something to say." A generation of pitches, the launchpad for Breathe Mongolia. Currently paused while we restructure for a stronger relaunch with bigger seed funding and dedicated mentorship.

Three project presentations and UNDP Mongolia's Blue Gold screening. The cohort spun out Breathe Mongolia, the air-quality campaign that has exhibited at every summit since. NHUB Lead: Jantsankhorol Damdinsuren. Head Judge: Galbadrakh Gombosuren. →
