About 2023

Resilience as a practice, not a posture.

South Florida · The Bass · Building Resilience · 8th annual.

The 2023 program took on the question every young Mongolian abroad was wrestling with after the pandemic: how do you rebuild a career, a community, a sense of self after two years of disruption? Speakers and panels explored the science of resilience, the cultural roots of nomadic adaptability, financial literacy in a tough economy, and the emotional vocabulary needed to ask for help.

Tserenna Erdenebileg (Morgan Stanley Managing Director and CFO of the global Institutional Equity Division) opened the program. The "Mongolians in Cultural Transition" panel paired Ideruugan Galbaatar (Georgetown / World Bank), Sodontuya Nerguidavaa (UC Davis MBA, CFA charter holder), and Badmaa Purevjav (CPA, Assurance Manager at PwC Chicago). Surenmaa (Brown University Mindfulness Center) led the goal-setting and stress-management workshops across both days.

The 2023 program

Two days, built around
the four R's.

Reflect → Reframe → Recharge → Restart. A four-stage arc across two days, each session designed to give attendees a usable mental model for the post-pandemic restart. Miami Beach Convention Center area; Collins Park lunches.

Day 01 · Saturday, March 4

Reflect on the journey. Reframe the challenge. Day one paired self-reflection with cultural-transition tools, keynotes opened the floor, workshops handed attendees a goal-setting template, and the cultural-transition panel gave language to the in-between space.

9:00 AM · Welcome & introduction
Opening from the MNG Summit board. Framing of the four-R arc and the day's pace.
10:00 AM · Keynote: reflecting on the journey
Tserenna Erdenebileg, Managing Director at Morgan Stanley and CFO of the global Institutional Equity Division. On identifying personal strengths and weaknesses through structured reflection, what resilience actually requires post-pandemic.
11:00 AM · Workshop: self-reflection & goal-setting
Facilitated by Surenmaa, MBSR meditation teacher certified by the Brown University Mindfulness Center. Attendees walked out with a written goal-setting document.
12:00 PM · Lunch & networking at Collins Park
Outdoor picnic-style lunch, weather permitting. Attendees brought their goals doc to the table.
1:00 PM · Panel: "Mongolians in Cultural Transition"
Featuring Ideruugan Galbaatar (Georgetown / World Bank), Sodontuya Nerguidavaa (UC Davis MBA, CFA charter holder), and Badmaa Purevjav (CPA, PwC Chicago). On the realities of building a U.S. career as a Mongolian, language, business culture, and the specific code-switches that drain energy.
2:00 PM · Workshop: communication & negotiation
Practical session on workplace communication, salary negotiation, and managing up.
3:00 PM · Keynote: reframing challenges as opportunities
Closing the day with a mindset talk on how to convert obstacles into next steps.

Day 02 · Sunday, March 5

Recharge the system. Then actually restart. Day two opened with self-care and mindfulness tools, anchored by the NHUB project competition presentations, and closed with a leadership workshop and an action-planning session.

9:00 AM · Keynote: self-care & positive mindset
The case for treating mental health as a daily discipline, not a crisis intervention.
10:00 AM · Workshop: mindfulness & stress management
Hands-on techniques attendees could use the same week.
11:00 AM · NHUB project competition presentations
"Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна", the cohort presented their projects to a live audience and judging panel.
12:00 PM · Lunch & networking
Industry mixers continue from Day 1.
1:00 PM · Workshop: leadership & decision-making
A panel on diversity, inclusion, and the daily decisions that shape a team's culture.
2:00 PM · Keynote: resilience & perseverance
The closing voice of the program, a story of building forward through ambiguity.
3:00 PM · Workshop: restart, the action-planning session
Translate Day 1's reflection and Day 2's recharge into a 30-day action plan, signed and shared with an accountability partner from the room.
4:00 PM · Networking & closing remarks
Final mixer. Mentorship-pair sign-up table. The 2024 SF date was teased here.

The 2023 speakers.

Verified against the live mngsummit.org/miamisummit-1 page. Roles below as of March 2023.

Keynote

T
Keynote
Tserenna Erdenebileg
Managing Director, Morgan Stanley · CFO for the global Institutional Equity Division. Joined the firm in 2002. BA in Accounting, Baruch College (2001); MBA, New York University (2006). Opened the program with a reflection on personal strengths, structured self-assessment, and what resilience actually requires after the pandemic years.

Panel · "Mongolians in Cultural Transition"

I
Panelist · Policy
Ideruugan Galbaatar
Second-year Master of Public Policy at Georgetown's McCourt School + consultant at the World Bank. Previously at the Public Affairs Section of the U.S. Embassy in Mongolia, where he implemented multiple projects to advance U.S. foreign policy in the East Asia Pacific region. BS Aircraft Maintenance Engineering, Mongolian University of Science and Technology; BA Business Administration, University of Finance and Economics of Mongolia.
S
Panelist · Entrepreneurship
Sodontuya Nerguidavaa
Mongolian nomad, youngest CFA charter holder, entrepreneur, publisher, former judo wrestler and basketball player. Named one of the 100 Best & Brightest MBAs worldwide by Poets & Quants in 2022. UC Davis MBA. Through her startup in Mongolia, personally trained over 500 industry professionals for the CFA designation exams. Published her autobiography in Mongolian.
B
Panelist · Assurance
Badmaa Purevjav
CPA. Assurance Manager at PwC, Chicago, with 6.5 years of experience across the Pharmaceuticals & Life Sciences, Financial Services, and Industrial Products sectors. Master of Accounting Science (Leadership) and BS Accountancy from Northern Illinois University.

Workshop · Stress management

S
Workshop facilitator
Surenmaa
Mindfulness-Based Stress Reduction meditation teacher certified by the Brown University Mindfulness Center. Pursuing an MPH in Epidemiology at Brown University School of Public Health. Previously at Kaiser, Gilead Sciences, and UCSF, working on gene therapy clinical trials and mental-health research. Led the goal-setting and mindfulness tracks across both days.
