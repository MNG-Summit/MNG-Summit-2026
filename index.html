About the Summit

We are a movement of nomads, building careers far from home.

MNG Summit exists to empower, encourage, and engage the next generation of Mongolians to reach their fullest personal and professional potential. We do this through one annual flagship gathering, year-round programs, and an ever-growing community of mentors, founders, scholars, and changemakers.

Twelve years in, we've watched our attendees become CEOs and policy analysts, climate-change writers and venture investors. Our power is in our people, and our promise is to keep showing up for them. Established 2014. A 501(c)(3) non-profit, run entirely by volunteers.

NHUB.

Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна.

"I have something to say."

NHUB is MNG Summit's annual project competition, a seed-funding contest for young Mongolians anywhere in the world who have an idea worth building, and the conviction to ship it. Running since 2011 (predating the Summit itself), our awardees have launched air-quality campaigns, mental-health platforms, education tools, and policy interventions across Mongolia and the diaspora.

Applications open every fall. Finalists pitch on the MNG Summit stage. Winners get $1,000–$1,500 in seed funding plus a year of mentorship to actually do the work.
