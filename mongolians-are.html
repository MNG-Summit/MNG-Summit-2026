About the series

"You can't be what you can't see."

Est. Aug 2020 · Virtual & free · Open globally.

"Mongolians Are…" started in August 2020, pandemic year, when the in-person Summit was paused and the team needed a way to keep showing up for the community. The premise: take one career track, find three Mongolians who've gone deep in it, and put them in a room together for an hour of breakout-room Q&A. No big stage. No keynote theatrics. Just our people, talking about the real work.

What started as a stopgap turned into one of MNG Summit's most-watched programs. Ten-plus episodes later, the series has profiled Mongolians at the Federal Reserve, Morgan Stanley, the Asia Foundation, Oyu Tolgoi, BBC and National Geographic productions, the Zorig Foundation, and the Obama Foundation. The format hasn't changed, because it works.
