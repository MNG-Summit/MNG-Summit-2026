"Mongolians
Are…"

A flagship webinar series profiling Mongolians across nine career tracks. Monthly. Wednesdays. Same simple format, every time.

Since 2020 Format 30-min talk + 30-min Q&A Cadence Monthly, Wednesdays

"You can't be
what you can't see."

The series exists because most young Mongolians have never met a Mongolian doing the job they want to do. The "Mongolians Are…" series puts one in front of them every month.

Past episodes have profiled lawyers, designers, founders, doctors, climate scientists, film directors, and policy advisors.
