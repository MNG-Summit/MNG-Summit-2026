Summit IX · 10th Anniversary · 2024

2024, San Francisco, United for Impact.

Ten years from a Chicago career fair, MNG Summit returned to the Bay Area for its biggest gathering yet, a three-day program at the University of San Francisco that doubled as a celebration of a decade of the Mongolian diaspora coming together.

About 2024

A milestone, marked the right way.

10th Anniversary · USF McLaren · United for Impact · 9th annual, 10 years in.

The 2024 summit's theme, "United for Impact", wasn't just a tagline. It was a directive: acknowledge our individual selves and leadership, and the power we have to influence the lives of others. The program brought speakers focused on collaborative solutions for a better future in business, society, and the environment.

Three days at McLaren Conference Center on the University of San Francisco campus, with hotel accommodations at the Hyatt Regency at Embarcadero. Attendees flew in from across the U.S., Mongolia, Canada, and Europe. Tickets ran $100 student / $150 early-bird / $200 regular. The 10th anniversary gala featured DJ BEME spinning a fusion of dark techno and Mongolian cultural sounds, a small detail that captured the whole vibe.

The 2024 program

Three days,
built around impact.

10th Anniversary, themed United for Impact, at the McLaren Conference Center, University of San Francisco. Reproduced with confirmed speaker affiliations as listed at the time of the summit.

Day 00 · Friday, March 15

Optional Bay Area tour day. Friday was for arrival, Bay Area exposure, and the soft open. Attendees met in the Hyatt Regency lobby at Embarcadero and chose between a coastal hike and curated company tours, capped by a casual Polk Street happy hour.

8:00 AM – 12:00 PM · Rodeo Beach coastal hike
Marin Headlands trail, group transport from the hotel. The kind of morning that turns strangers into a cohort.
1:30 PM · Salesforce HQ tour
Behind-the-scenes visit hosted by the SF Salesforce team. A first look at the company that closed Day 2 with VP Kevin Francis on stage.
2:30 PM · Amazon SF office tour
Guided visit, hosted by Amazon's Mongolian alumni network.
6:00 PM · Networking happy hour at SF Brewing Company
3150 Polk Street. Optional, casual, the room you'd see again the next morning at registration.

Day 01 · Saturday, March 16

Lead the journey. Reflect on the path. Day one opened with a Consul General address, an opening keynote on leadership, two thematic panels, Open Mic storytelling, and a wellbeing session, closing with a Hyatt Waterfront-Room happy hour.

8:30 AM · Registration & coffee
McLaren Conference Center, University of San Francisco. Complimentary parking at the Hayes Healy Lot.
9:30 AM · Opening & welcome remarks
Batchimeg Ganbaatar (Board Chair, MNG Summit · Co-Founder & Managing Partner, Nomadic Venture Partners) · Munkhbaatar Begzjav (Consul General of Mongolia in San Francisco) · Bold Baatar (CE Copper, Member of ExCo, Rio Tinto, virtual).
11:00 AM · Keynote: Leadership
Otgo Erhemjamts, Dean, School of Management, University of San Francisco.
11:40 AM · Panel: Lead the Journey
Tsolmon Jargalsaikhan (Director of Finance, Kindred Ventures) · Luvsanbyamba Buyankhuu Luba (Founder, Boost) · Gerelmaa Batchuluun (CEO, Steppe Group). Moderated by Amarlin Anand (Seller and Governance Specialist, TikTok).
12:40 PM · Lunch & networking
McLaren atrium. Industry-coded tables.
1:30 PM · Guest keynote: How AI can accelerate the green transition
Frank Meehan, CPO, Improvability AI.
1:50 PM · Panel: Future of Sustainability
Baigalmaa Chinbat (Hubert H. Humphrey Fellow, UC Davis · Head of Finance, Clean Energy LLC) · Azjargal Tsogtsaikhan (Founder & CEO, Breathe Mongolia) · Bulgan Khaltar (Management Consultant, Arcadis). Moderated by Zaya Delgerjargal (M.A. Environmental Management, Yale).
3:00 PM · Open Mic: "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна"
Tegshee Baasan (Founder & Host, Unlock Podcast) · Urga Azat (Board Member, DEM Foundation) · Ariunzayat Yunren (Founder & CEO, esportsnews.mn).
3:30 PM · Wellbeing & mental health, in conversation with Khaliun
Khaliun Jargalsaikhan, licensed therapist. Themes: personal growth, work–life balance, and mindfulness for the Mongolian diaspora.
7:00 PM · Networking happy hour at the Hyatt Waterfront Room
5 Embarcadero Center. The conversations the panels started, finished here.

Day 02 · Sunday, March 17

Navigate the digital frontier. Restart with skills. Day two opened with Salesforce VP Kevin Francis, then a panel on emerging technology, a second Open Mic block, and two practical workshops. The 10th anniversary closed where it started: in conversation.

10:00 AM · Guest keynote: Salesforce
Kevin Francis, Vice President, Salesforce.
11:00 AM · Panel: Navigating the Digital Frontier
Tsenguun Tsogbadrakh (Founder & CEO, Pypup · Senior Engineer, TikTok) · Khongorzul Bat-Ireedui (MSc., Learning Design & Technology, Stanford) · Batchimeg Ganbaatar (Board Chair, MNG Summit). Moderated by Badmaa Purevjav (Audit Manager, Motorola Solutions).
12:00 PM · Lunch & networking
Industry pods continue from Day 1 lunch.
1:00 PM · Open Mic: "Надад Хэлэх Үг Байна"
Batsoyombo Ts. (President, Bay Area Mongolian Community Association) · Tselmeg (Seattle, on learning) · Badmaasambuu Tsendayush (Financial Controller, Vivi) · Enkhhuslen Baterdene (Public Policy, Duke) · Gombo Batsukh (Sales, Michigan) · Suvd Davaadorj (Medical Devices, Tuna Scholarship) · Nyamjargal Lhagva (Filmmaker, Amina Project).
2:00 PM · Workshop: Crafting your career story
A resume workshop with Tsenguun Tsogbadrakh (Founder & CEO, Pypup · Senior Engineer, TikTok).
2:45 PM · Workshop: Technical interview patterns
Navigating pitfalls and cultivating the right mindset, with Enkhtur Narantungalag (Software Engineer, Google).
3:30 PM · Closing: group activity & stay-connected
Send-off ritual, mentorship sign-up table, and the gala kickoff with DJ BEME, dark techno × Mongolian fusion to close the 10th anniversary.
